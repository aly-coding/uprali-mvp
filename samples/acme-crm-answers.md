# Acme Analytics: build our own CRM or keep HubSpot? (fictional)

Acme Analytics is a made-up 40-person B2B SaaS company. Its PM wants to know whether one engineer should build a simple in-house CRM.

Paste this into the decision box, or send it as `question`:

> Should we build our own CRM or keep paying $720 a year for HubSpot Starter? Our engineer built a working prototype in 3 weeks. It would need 2 integrations, email and billing.

Expected memo: **Buy HubSpot Starter**. The prototype looks like $12,000 of work, but it's only about 20% of the job. Built, hardened and run for 3 years, the in-house CRM costs about $200,000 against about $17,000 to keep paying for HubSpot. Status is **draft**, because the engineer's weekly cost and the value of core product work are still benchmarks that a person should confirm.
