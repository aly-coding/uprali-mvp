# Acme Analytics: build our own CRM or keep HubSpot? (fictional)

Acme Analytics is a made-up 40-person B2B SaaS company. Its PM wants to know if one engineer should build a simple in-house CRM.

Pick **Build vs buy** and answer the analyst like this:

| The analyst asks about | You answer |
|---|---|
| Which tool or vendor would you buy? | HubSpot Starter |
| What would it cost a year? | about $720 a year |
| Any one-time setup cost? | 0 |
| How many engineer-weeks to build? | 2 weeks |
| What does an engineer cost per week? | not sure |
| Yearly maintenance as a share of build cost? | 20% |
| Over how many years should we compare? | 3 |

Expected memo: **Buy HubSpot Starter**. Building costs about $13k over 3 years, against $2,160 for buying. The engineer cost is a benchmark of $4,000 a week, marked low confidence.
