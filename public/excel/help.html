Uprali for Excel
How to load the add-in and use it

Step 1: get the add-in file

Download the add-in file. It's a small text file that tells Excel where Uprali lives.

Download uprali-excel-manifest.xml

Excel only uses this file to find Uprali. Nothing in your workbook leaves Excel until you press Build or Explain in the Uprali panel.

Excel on the web (easiest, try this first)

  1. Go to excel.cloud.microsoft (or office.com, then Excel) and open any workbook, or a new blank one.
  2. On the Home tab, click Add-ins, then More Add-ins. On some accounts it's Insert, then Add-ins, then More Add-ins.
  3. In the window that opens, click My Add-ins, then Upload My Add-in (top right).
  4. Click Browse, pick uprali-excel-manifest.xml from your Downloads, and click Upload.
  5. An Uprali models button appears on the Home tab. Click it to open the panel.

If there's no Upload My Add-in, your work or school account blocks custom add-ins. Use a personal Microsoft account, or ask your Microsoft 365 admin.

Excel on a Mac

  1. Quit Excel.
  2. In Finder, click Go, then Go to Folder…, paste ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Excel/Data/Documents/ and press Return.
  3. If there's no folder named wef there, make one (right click, New Folder, name it wef).
  4. Drag uprali-excel-manifest.xml into the wef folder.
  5. Open Excel and any workbook. On the Home tab click Add-ins (or Insert, then My Add-ins), and pick Uprali under Developer Add-ins.

Excel on Windows

  1. Make a folder, for example C:\UpraliAddin, and put uprali-excel-manifest.xml in it.
  2. Right click the folder, choose Properties, then the Sharing tab, then Share…, then Share. Copy the network path it shows, like \\YOUR-PC\UpraliAddin.
  3. In Excel: File, Options, Trust Center, Trust Center Settings…, Trusted Add-in Catalogs.
  4. Paste the network path into Catalog Url, click Add catalog, tick Show in Menu, then OK. Close and reopen Excel.
  5. Home, then Add-ins, then More Add-ins (or Insert, then My Add-ins). Open the Shared Folder tab, pick Uprali, click Add.

Needs Excel for Microsoft 365 or Excel 2021 and later.

Using it

  1. In the panel, pick a model: Build vs buy, New product launch, ROI on an AI investment or Unit economics. Fill in the boxes marked with a star, and anything else you know.
  2. Click Build the sheet. A new sheet appears with the model.
  3. Yellow cells are inputs. Change any of them and every total and the recommendation update.
  4. Click Explain this sheet for Uprali's notes. AI notes are marked Suggestion, verify: check them before you rely on them.
